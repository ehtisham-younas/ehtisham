Hospital Management Portal (HMP)

High-Level Requirements

1. Patient Access & Management

The HMP shall provide a secure, self-service platform enabling patients to manage their personal profile, schedule appointments, and

access their medical records and visit summaries.

2. Doctor Workflow & Records

The HMP shall provide doctors with tools to manage their clinical schedules, view and update assigned patient medical records (EHR),

and track pending appointment requests.

3. Administrative Oversight

The HMP shall provide administrators with a comprehensive dashboard to manage system users (patients and doctors), oversee all

appointment activity, and maintain system data integrity.

4. Secure Communication

The HMP shall implement a secure, encrypted messaging channel to facilitate confidential, auditable communication between patients

and their assigned doctors.

5. Data Security & Compliance

The HMP shall ensure full compliance with HIPAA and national data protection laws, including strong authentication, role-based access

control (RBAC), and encryption of all Protected Health Information (PHI) at rest and in transit.

6. Performance & Reliability

The HMP shall be a highly available platform (the uptime) capable of handling concurrent user loads and providing a fast, intuitive user

experience.

7. System Auditability

The HMP shall maintain a comprehensive audit log of all significant user actions, including logins, PHI access, and record modifications,

for security and compliance purposes.

8. Reporting & Analytics

The HMP shall provide configurable dashboards and reports for administrators to monitor appointments, user engagement, and

communication metrics, with exportable data formats (CSV/PDF) for analysis and compliance tracking.

9. Accessibility & Localization

The HMP shall comply with WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) 2.1 AA accessibility standards, ensuring usability for all

users, and support multiple languages, date/time formats, and regional localization settings.

10. Integrations & APIs

The HMP shall offer RESTful and/or FHIR-based APIs to integrate with existing hospital systems such as EHR, LIS, and insurance

platforms, as well as webhook notifications for appointment and messaging events.

11. Configuration & Operations

The HMP shall support configurable settings such as hospital hours, holidays, and policies; provide feature flags for controlled rollouts;

and maintain an auditable record of configuration or deployment changes.

12. Legal, Privacy & Consent

The HMP shall include Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and consent management workflows; enable patients to exercise rights such as

access, correction, or deletion of data; and display cookie/consent banners where required by law.

13. Acceptance Criteria

The HMP shall enforce multi-factor authentication, maintain verifiable uptime, ensure immutable audit logs, and achieve measurable

improvements in appointment management efficiency and patient engagement.
